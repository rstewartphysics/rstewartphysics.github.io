LED protective resistor practice

Work out the protective resistor using: R = (Vs − Vf) / I (with I in amps). Enter your resistor value in ohms, then press Check my answer.

Hints (tap to open)
  • Convert mA to A: divide by 1000 (e.g. 20 mA = 0.020 A).
  • If Vs ≤ Vf, the LED won’t light correctly.
  • In real life you choose the nearest sensible standard resistor value.

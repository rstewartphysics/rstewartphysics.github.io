Overview

A simple revision plan
  • 1) Learn the key ideas — short notes and videos.
  • 2) Practise calculations until they feel automatic.
  • 3) Do past papers by topic to spot weak areas fast.
  • 4) Repeat little and often — confidence comes from reps.
Higher Physics outline
  • Electricity: measurement uncertainty, I–V characteristics, internal resistance, capacitors, AC/DC, semiconductors and band theory.
  • Our Dynamic Universe: vectors, motion, forces, momentum, gravitation, special relativity, expanding universe.
  • Particles & Waves: refraction/interference, spectra, photoelectric effect, nuclear reactions, and the Standard Model.
  • Assignment: planning a fair test, collecting quality data, analysing, and justifying a conclusion.
SQA marking principles
  • Calculations: write the relationship, substitute with units, then give the final answer with a unit.
  • Show questions: show the working, not just the final number.
  • Explain / justify: link cause to effect clearly and precisely.
  • Graphs: label axes with quantity and unit, use a sensible scale, and draw one best-fit line or curve.
  • Point questions: if it asks for 1 or 2 points, do not add extras.

Core

13 marks

The demand of the real test — one rearrangement, one conversion or one intermediate step.

66 marks

Antares is a red supergiant in the constellation Scorpius. It is 5.3 × 10¹⁸ m from Earth. Its apparent brightness is 8.5 × 10⁻⁸ W m⁻².

(a) Calculate the luminosity of Antares.3

(b) The radius of Antares is 4.7 × 10¹¹ m. Calculate the surface temperature of Antares.3

77 marks

Tau Ceti is a Sun-like star. Its surface temperature is 5340 K and its radius is 5.52 × 10⁸ m.

(a) Calculate the luminosity of Tau Ceti.3

(b) Tau Ceti is 11.9 light-years from Earth. Determine the apparent brightness of Tau Ceti seen from Earth.4

82 marks

Star X is marked on the H-R diagram. The dashed lines join stars that have the same radius.

(a) State the type of star X.1

92 marks

Deneb and Mu Cephei are both supergiants. Their luminosities are about the same. Deneb has a surface temperature of 8.5 × 10³ K. Mu Cephei has a surface temperature of 3.8 × 10³ K.

State whether the radius of Mu Cephei is greater than, less than or equal to the radius of Deneb. Justify your answer.2

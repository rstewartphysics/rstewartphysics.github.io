Section 2

20 marks

Longer answers. Where a question says explain, say what happens and why.

118 marks

A new hospital is to be built on the edge of Edinburgh. It will have operating theatres, lifts, a heating system and solar panels on the roof. Building work will take three years.

(a) Name the type of engineer who would design the hospital's lighting and power supply.1

(b) Describe one task a mechanical engineer would carry out on the hospital.1

(c) Describe one positive effect the hospital could have on patients, and one negative effect the building work could have on people living nearby.2

(d) Describe one positive and one negative economic effect of building the hospital.2

(e) Give a real example of an engineered system in Scotland that reduces harm to the environment, and describe how it does this.2

128 marks

The hospital has electric hand dryers in its toilets and electric motors to drive its lifts.

(a) In your jotter, draw an energy transfer diagram for an electric hand dryer. Show the input energy, the useful output energy and one form of wasted energy.2

Draw this in your jotter. When you have finished, reveal the mark scheme and compare your drawing with the model answer.

(b) One lift motor transfers 96 000 J of energy in 16 s. Calculate its power.2

(c) Engineers want the lift motors to be as efficient as possible. Give two reasons why.2

(d) The hand dryers are fitted with sensors so that they switch off when no one is using them. Explain how this helps the environment.1

(e) Explain why heat is a useful output of the hand dryer but a wasted output of the lift motor.1

134 marks

A rollercoaster works like a very fast marble run. A motor pulls the car up the chain lift to the top of the first hill, then the car rolls down and over smaller hills to the end of the ride.

(a) Name the form of energy the car has at the top of the first hill.1

(b) Describe the energy change as the car goes down the first drop, and explain why each hill must be lower than the one before.2

(c) The chain lift is driven by an electric motor. State the input energy and the useful output energy of the motor.1

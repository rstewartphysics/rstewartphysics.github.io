3/2 roller trip valve

Pneumatic components

← All pneumatic components

Symbol

What it does

One way only. Pushed one way, the roller works the valve. Air flows from port 1 to port 2.

Pushed the other way, the hinge folds. Nothing happens.

Key words

3/2 valve
A valve with 3 ports and 2 positions.
Actuator
The part that works the valve.
Port
A numbered hole where a pipe joins.
Signal
Air sent to make the next part work.
Mechanical
Worked by a moving part.
Roller trip
A roller that works the valve one way only.

What it does

Main air comes from a compressor. It feeds the whole circuit.

The regulator sets the air pressure. The gauge shows it in bar.

Pipes push onto the air outlets.

Key words

Main air
The air supply that feeds the circuit.
Pressure
How hard the air pushes. Measured in bar.
Regulator
The knob that sets the air pressure.
Gauge
The dial that shows the air pressure.

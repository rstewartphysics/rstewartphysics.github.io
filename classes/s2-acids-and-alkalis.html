S2 Chemistry – Acids and Alkalis

🧪 S2 Chemistry — Acids & Alkalis

This page is a quick, clear summary of the must-know ideas + a few interactive tools. Use it to practise: pH, indicators, and neutralisation.

Safety: acids and alkalis can be irritant or corrosive. Wear eye protection in practical work, and wash hands after.

Key ideas (short + clear)

Acid

  • An acid has a pH below 7.
  • Acids often taste sour (do not taste in class).
  • Acids can react with metals and carbonates.

Alkali (a base in water)

  • An alkali has a pH above 7.
  • Alkalis can feel slippery (but can still burn skin).
  • Some alkalis are used in cleaning products.

Neutral

  • Neutral means pH = 7.
  • Pure water is neutral.
  • “Neutral” is not always “safe” (some substances are still harmful).

Key words

  • pH (0 to 14)
  • indicator (colour shows pH)
  • neutralisation (acid + alkali)
  • corrosive / irritant

Helpful examples

  • pH 1–3: strong acid (e.g. stomach acid)
  • pH 4–6: weak acid (e.g. vinegar / fizzy drinks)
  • pH 7: neutral (pure water)
  • pH 8–10: weak alkali (e.g. baking soda solution)
  • pH 11–14: strong alkali (e.g. bleach / drain cleaner)

Quick reminder

  • Universal indicator shows a wide range of colours for different pH values.
  • Litmus: red in acid, blue in alkali, purple in neutral.
  • Phenolphthalein: colourless in acid/neutral, pink in alkali.
  • Methyl orange: red in acid, orange in neutral, yellow in alkali.

Neutralisation (interactive equation builder)

Neutralisation is when an acid reacts with an alkali to make salt and water.

Build a word equation

Press Build to generate the equation.
⭐ Pattern to remembereasy marks
acid + alkali → salt + water

The salt name comes from: (acid gives the second part) + (metal from the alkali).

  • Hydrochloric acid makes chloride salts.
  • Sulfuric acid makes sulfate salts.
  • Nitric acid makes nitrate salts.

Fast practical meaning

  • Neutralisation can reduce acidity (e.g. antacids).
  • It is used to treat soil that is too acidic.
  • In a titration, you add one solution until the indicator changes colour.

Extended response ready (perfect sentences)

These are the kind of points that score marks quickly. Learn a few for each topic.

🦴 Skeletal system Functions
  • The skeleton supports the body and gives it shape.
  • Bones protect delicate organs (e.g. skull protects the brain).
  • Bones provide attachment points for muscles so the body can move.
  • Bone marrow produces blood cells.
  • Tendons connect muscle to bone; ligaments connect bone to bone at joints.
🫁 Respiratory system Gas exchange
  • We breathe in oxygen and breathe out carbon dioxide.
  • Gas exchange happens in the alveoli in the lungs.
  • Alveoli are good for gas exchange because they have a large surface area, thin walls and a good blood supply.
  • When you inhale, the chest expands and the diaphragm contracts (moves down).
  • Carbon dioxide test: limewater turns cloudy.
❤️ Circulatory system Heart + vessels
  • The heart is a muscle that pumps blood around the body.
  • Arteries carry blood away from the heart and have thick walls for high pressure.
  • Veins carry blood back to the heart and have valves to stop backflow.
  • Capillaries are tiny vessels where exchange happens (e.g. oxygen and carbon dioxide).
🍽️ Digestive system Peristalsis
  • Digestion breaks large insoluble food molecules into smaller soluble molecules for energy and growth.
  • Peristalsis is wave-like muscle contractions in the oesophagus that push food to the stomach.
  • Enzymes speed up the breakdown of food molecules during digestion.
  • The stomach churns food with acid and enzymes to break it down further.
  • Bile helps to break down fats.
🧬 Reproduction Organs
  • Reproduction is producing offspring.
  • Sexual reproduction needs two parents; asexual reproduction needs one parent.
  • Male organs include: testes, sperm tube/duct, penis, urethra, prostate, seminal vesicle.
  • Female organs include: ovaries, oviduct, uterus (womb), cervix, vagina.
  • Human sex cells are sperm and egg.
🥚 Fertilisation + pregnancy Zygote
  • Fertilisation is when the sperm nucleus joins with the egg nucleus to form a zygote.
  • The zygote divides to form an embryo as it moves to the uterus.
  • Implantation is when the embryo attaches to the uterus wall.
  • The placenta allows food/oxygen to pass to the baby and waste to pass to the mother.
  • The umbilical cord connects the baby to the placenta.
  • Pregnancy lasts about 40 weeks.
⚠️ Keeping the baby healthy Safety
  • Harmful substances from smoking, alcohol and some drugs can pass from the mother’s blood to the baby.

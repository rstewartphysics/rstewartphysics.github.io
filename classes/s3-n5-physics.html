Overview

National 5 Physics is where the world starts to feel explainable. You learn to spot the pattern, choose the right equation, and prove your thinking with evidence.

It’s not “memorise and hope” — it’s “model, test, improve”… like a scientist with a calculator.

How to use this page: start with the resources below — then use past papers to train exam confidence.

✅ A simple revision plan (tap to open)doable
  • 1) Learn the key ideas (short notes + videos).
  • 2) Practise calculations until they feel automatic.
  • 3) Do “past papers by topic” to spot weak areas quickly.
  • 4) Repeat little and often — confidence comes from reps.

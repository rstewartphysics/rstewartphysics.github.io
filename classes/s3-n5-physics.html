Overview

S3 Physics is National 5 level work. S3 and N5 share this page, topic by topic. What you learn in S3 carries straight on into National 5.

A weekly routine that works
  • 1) Go over the lesson on the topic page or in the slides.
  • 2) Do the tasks, then check your answers.
  • 3) Try past-paper questions, then fix what you got wrong.
  • Stuck? Write down what you know, the equation and the units before you use the calculator.
How marks are given
  • Calculations: formula, substitution, final answer with unit.
  • Show questions: show all working.
  • Explain: link cause to effect, clearly and simply.
  • Graphs: label axes and units, sensible scale, clear line.

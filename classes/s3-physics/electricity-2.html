📘 Electricity 2 – Notes & Revision

This block focuses on electronics, sensing, control and switching circuits.

🔢 L1–L3 Potential Dividers (with Worked Examples)

V2 = (R2 / (R1 + R2)) × Vs

Worked Example – Equal Resistors

  • Vs = 6.0 V
  • R1 = 1.0 kΩ
  • R2 = 1.0 kΩ

V2 = (1000 / 2000) × 6.0 = 3.0 V

Worked Example – Unequal Resistors

  • Vs = 6.0 V
  • R1 = 1.2 kΩ
  • R2 = 150 Ω

V2 = (150 / 1350) × 6.0 = 0.67 V

💡 L4 Diodes & LEDs (Worked Example)

LED Protective Resistor

  • Vs = 5.0 V
  • VLED = 2.0 V
  • I = 10 mA (0.010 A)

VR = 5.0 − 2.0 = 3.0 V

R = 3.0 / 0.010 = 300 Ω

Choose 330 Ω

🧾 Stripboard Schematics – Basic Transistor Circuit
  • Draw component positions, not symbols
  • Copper tracks run in straight lines
  • Track cuts prevent short circuits
  • Transistor legs must be on separate tracks
  • Power rails must be labelled
🔍 Fault Finding – When Circuits Don’t Work

Visual Checks (Power OFF)

  • Correct component orientation?
  • Solder bridges?
  • Missing track cuts?

Power & Signal Checks

  • Is the supply present?
  • Does the input voltage change?
  • Does the transistor reach switching voltage?

Common Faults

  • LED never on → reversed LED or no resistor
  • Always on → base voltage too high
  • No response → divider wired incorrectly

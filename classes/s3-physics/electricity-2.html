📘 Electricity 2 – Notes & Revision

This block focuses on electronics, sensing, control and switching circuits.

🗺️ What this assessment is about
  • Potential dividers
  • Diodes and LEDs
  • Sensors and special components
  • Control and switching circuits
  • Soldering and construction
🔢 L1–L3 Potential Dividers

V₂ = (R₂ / (R₁ + R₂)) × Vs

  • Used to produce a fraction of the supply voltage
  • Foundation of sensing and control circuits
  • Always compare calculated, simulated and measured values
💡 L4 Diodes & LEDs
  • Diodes allow current in one direction
  • LEDs emit light when forward biased
  • Protective resistors prevent damage
🌡️ L5 Special Components
  • LDR: ↑ light → ↓ resistance
  • Thermistor: ↑ temperature → ↓ resistance
  • Capacitor stores charge
🎛️ L6 Control Circuits
  • Low / High light circuits
  • Low / High temperature circuits
  • Variable resistors set switching point
🔄 L7 Switching Circuits
  • NPN transistor & MOSFET
  • Used as electronic switches
  • Switch on at a threshold voltage
🔧 L8–L9 Soldering & Build
  • Stripboard soldering
  • Track cutter, heat sink, solder sucker
  • Design and build own circuit
📝 Success Criteria
  • I can calculate potential divider voltages
  • I can simulate circuits using Yenka
  • I can calculate LED protective resistors
  • I can explain sensor behaviour
  • I can build control and switching circuits
  • I can solder safely

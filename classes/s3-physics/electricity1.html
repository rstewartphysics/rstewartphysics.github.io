📌 Core Facts & Equations
Key definitions
  • Voltage (V) – energy per coulomb. Unit: **volt (V)**
  • Current (I) – charge per second. Unit: **ampere (A)**
  • Resistance (R) – opposition to current. Unit: **ohm (Ω)**
Essential equations
  • V = I R
  • Series resistance: RT = R₁ + R₂ + …
  • Parallel resistance: 1/RT = 1/R₁ + 1/R₂ + …
  • Series circuits: currents same; voltages share
  • Parallel circuits: voltages same; currents share

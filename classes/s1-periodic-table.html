    Practicals (quick summary of what you did)

    This is just a recap — for the test, focus on the correct method names and key ideas.

    🔥 Flame testing metalsidentify metals
    • Observed different flame colours for different metal salts.
    • Used observations as evidence to identify or compare metals.
    💥 Alkali metals demo (Group 1)reactivity trend
    • Observed lithium, sodium and potassium reacting with water.
    • Used evidence (fizzing, heat, flames) to describe the trend down the group.
    🧲 Separating iron + sulphur (mixture)magnet
    • Separated iron from sulphur using a magnet.
    • Conclusion: it’s a mixture because it can be separated physically.
    🌊 Separating sand + saltdissolve → filter → evaporate
    • Dissolve the salt, filter the sand, then evaporate to get salt back.
    🌈 Chromatography (inks)separate dyes
    • Separated ink into different dyes using paper and water.
    • Conclusion: inks are mixtures of dissolved substances.
    ⚗️ Making a compound (iron + sulphur heated)compound
    • Heated iron + sulphur to form iron sulphide.
    • Key idea: a compound has different properties from the original elements.

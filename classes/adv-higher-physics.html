Advanced Higher Physics hub

Overview

Higher in much greater depth: fewer topics, modelled mathematically, calculus included. Past-paper practice with corrections is what moves the grade.

What we study
  • Modelling: explanations built from assumptions, evidence and maths.
  • Advanced ideas: concepts that seem strange at first, but work.
  • Skills: clear derivations, tidy working, strong graphs and calm explanations.
  • Exam questions: define, model, calculate, interpret.
A weekly routine that works
  • 1) Learn the idea from the notes or a video.
  • 2) Do 2–3 worked examples.
  • 3) Do past-paper questions, then check and fix your mistakes.
  • Stuck? Write the knowns, the model and the equation before you use the calculator.

Overview

Advanced Higher Physics builds directly on Higher: you study a smaller set of topics in much greater depth, using mathematical models — including calculus — to explain and predict how things behave. It rewards careful, methodical working far more than memorising.

How to use this page: start with the four essential resources below, then do past-paper questions every week. In AH, regular question practice with corrections is what moves your grade.

🧠 What we study (big picture)
  • Serious modelling: building explanations from assumptions, evidence, and maths that actually means something.
  • Modern/advanced ideas: the kind of concepts that make you say “that’s weird… but it works.”
  • Skills focus: clear derivations, tidy working, strong graphs, and explanations that don’t panic under pressure.
  • Exam mindset: AH questions love structure — define, model, calculate, interpret.
Nerdy truth: AH is basically “be friends with the model” — once you trust it, the marks start appearing.
✅ A quick weekly routine that works
  • 1) Learn the idea (notes/video) → 2) do 2–3 worked examples → 3) past-paper questions → 4) check + fix mistakes.
  • When stuck: write the knowns, the model/assumption, and the equation before touching the calculator.
  • Use the Past Paper Question Finder tile as your main “training ground”.

Topics

One page per topic, in course order. The letter is the teaching order — the same letter the class deck carries, so the slides index and these pages line up. Pages are added as they are written.

Unit 1 — Rotational Motion & Astrophysics

a · Kinematics Displacement, velocity and acceleration as calculus. b · Angular Motion Angular displacement, velocity and acceleration. c · Rotational Dynamics Torque, moment of inertia and angular momentum. e · Gravitation Fields, potential, escape velocity and black holes. f · General Relativity Spacetime, equivalence and gravitational time dilation. g · Stellar Physics Luminosity, the H–R diagram and stellar evolution.

Unit 2 — Quanta and Waves

p · Quantum Theory Wave–particle duality, uncertainty and tunnelling. k · Particles from Space Cosmic rays, the solar wind and the aurora. d · Simple Harmonic Motion Definition, energy in SHM, damping and resonance. m · Waves The wave equation, phase, intensity and the Doppler effect. n · Interference Division of amplitude and division of wavefront. o · Polarisation Plane polarisation, Brewster's angle and applications.

Unit 3 — Electromagnetism

i · Electric Fields Coulomb's law, field patterns, potential and charged particles. j · Magnetic Fields Force on a conductor, B-fields and the definition of the amp. l · Circuits Capacitors, inductors, and the growth and decay of current. h · Electromagnetic Radiation Maxwell's equations and the nature of EM waves.

Researching Physics

0 · Uncertainties Scale, random and calibration uncertainty, and combining them.

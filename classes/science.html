Overview

Science is basically the art of asking “why?” — then refusing to accept the first answer until the evidence agrees. We’ll explore waves you can’t see, reactions you can’t un-mix, cells that behave like tiny cities, and forces that make the universe do its thing. If it can be measured, tested, and explained… it’s science (and yes, we do get to set stuff on fire safely).

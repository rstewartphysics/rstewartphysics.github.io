A student uses the circuit shown to determine the e.m.f. E and internal resistance r of a battery. The variable resistor provides known values of resistance R. For each value of R the switch is closed, the current I is noted and 1/I is calculated.
Applying conservation of energy to the circuit gives R = E × (1/I) − r, which is the equation of a straight line: the gradient is E and the intercept on the R axis is −r. The graph of the student's results is shown.

(a) Use the graph to determine the internal resistance of the battery.1

(b) Use the graph to determine the e.m.f. of the battery.2

📘 Course overview

Built to match your Higher Physics success criteria and the structure you’ve taught before.

🧠 Big ideas & units (what you’ll learn)
  • Our Dynamic Universe (ODU): motion, forces, energy, momentum, gravity/fields (as taught in your sequence).
  • Particles & Waves: inverse square law, refraction, interference, spectra, photoelectric effect, the standard model, nuclear reactions.
  • Electricity: circuit behaviour (including more advanced ideas like internal resistance / AC where appropriate), and using equations confidently.
  • Researching Physics (assignment): planning, processing data, uncertainties, and communicating findings clearly.

Note: the exact order can change year-to-year — the timeline below is your 25–26 plan.

📝 Assessment (how you’re graded)
  • Question paper: sat in one sitting with two papers (Paper 1 + Paper 2).
  • Assignment: Researching Physics report.
  • Best revision strategy: learn the idea → then do questions until it feels automatic.

The timeline includes a prelim window and the final SQA exam date.

🧰 Skills that matter all year
  • Using equations correctly (rearranging, units, significant figures).
  • Graph skills: gradient, intercepts, and what graphs mean physically.
  • Uncertainties: absolute/percentage uncertainty and sensible conclusions.
  • Explaining answers clearly (especially for written questions).

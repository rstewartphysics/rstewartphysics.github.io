Overview

Engineering Science is where ideas become real: you learn how systems work, how to model them, and how to improve them. It links design, electronics, mechanisms, energy, and problem solving.

Starts June 2026: S3 (largely National 4 style) and National 5. Higher is planned for June 2027.

How to use this page: choose your course below, then use the quick links for official docs and extra support.

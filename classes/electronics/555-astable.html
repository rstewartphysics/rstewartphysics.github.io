📘 How the 555 Astable Works (click to expand)

The capacitor charges through R1 and R2 until it reaches 2/3 of the supply. The 555 then forces the output LOW and the capacitor discharges through R2 only until it drops below 1/3 of the supply. This repeats, creating a square wave.

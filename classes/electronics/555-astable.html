Concept 1

How it works

A 555 astable switches its output, pin 3, between HIGH and LOW again and again, with nothing pressing a button. Capacitor C1 sets the rhythm. It charges and discharges, but only between ⅓ and ⅔ of the supply: 3 V and 6 V on a 9 V supply. It never fills up and never empties.

The red LED joins pin 3 to 0 V. It lights when pin 3 is HIGH.
The green LED joins +9 V to pin 3. It lights when pin 3 is LOW.
Watch out: R1 is only in the charging path. Changing R1 changes how long pin 3 stays HIGH, but not how long it stays LOW.

  • The 0.7 comes from the ⅓ to ⅔ swing. It is 0.693 rounded.
  • Mark time is always longer than space time in this circuit, because R1 + R2 is bigger than R2.
  • Doubling C1 doubles both times, so the frequency halves.
  • Convert first: kΩ → Ω (× 1000) and µF → F (÷ 1 000 000).

Practice

    Concept 3

    Build it on stripboard

    555 pins, seen from above

    • Find the notch. With the notch at the top, pin 1 is top left.
    • Count anticlockwise: down the left side 1 to 4, then up the right side 5 to 8.
    • Pin 8 is top right, opposite pin 1.
    Fit the chip and cut the tracks
    • Place the chip where the layout shows it, notch the same way round.
    • Make every track cut the layout marks with a red ✗. No two pins of the chip may share a copper track, or they are joined together.
    Power: pins 8, 4 and 1
    • Pin 8 and pin 4 go to +9 V.
    • Pin 1 goes to 0 V.
    • Pin 5 is not used in this circuit.
    The timing parts: R1, R2 and C1
    • R1 from +9 V to pin 7.
    • R2 from pin 7 to pins 2 and 6.
    • Link pin 2 to pin 6 with a wire.
    • C1 from pins 2 and 6 to 0 V. It is polarised: the long leg (+) goes to pins 2 and 6, the striped side to 0 V.
    The LEDs and their 680 Ω resistors
    • Red LED and a 680 Ω resistor from pin 3 to 0 V. The flat edge (short leg) faces 0 V.
    • Green LED and a 680 Ω resistor from +9 V to pin 3. The flat edge (short leg) faces pin 3.
    Check before you switch on
    • Pin 1 is at the notch end, top left.
    • Every marked track cut is made, and there are no solder bridges.
    • Pins 2 and 6 are linked.
    • C1 and both LEDs are the right way round.
    It doesn't flash: what to check
    • Nothing lights: check pins 8 and 4 reach +9 V and pin 1 reaches 0 V.
    • One LED stays on: check the link from pin 2 to pin 6, and that C1 is the right way round.
    • One LED never lights: that LED is probably the wrong way round.
    • Both look on all the time: it may be flashing too fast to see. Check C1's value.

    Words to know

    Astable
    Has no stable state: it switches HIGH and LOW by itself, again and again.
    Mark time
    How long the output stays HIGH in each cycle.
    Space time
    How long the output stays LOW in each cycle.
    Period
    One HIGH plus one LOW: mark time + space time.
    Frequency
    How many cycles happen each second, in hertz (Hz).
    Duty cycle
    The share of each cycle that the output is HIGH, as a percentage.

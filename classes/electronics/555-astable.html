How the 555 Astable Works

Important misconception: The capacitor does NOT fully charge or fully discharge. It only moves between 1/3 and 2/3 of the supply voltage.

📘 Click to expand explanation

The capacitor charges through R1 and R2 until it reaches 2/3 of the supply voltage. The 555 then switches LOW and the capacitor discharges through only R2 until it drops below 1/3. This loop repeats forever, creating a continuous square wave.

Try This!

These challenges help you explore how resistor and capacitor values control the timing. Misconception to avoid: R1 does NOT affect discharge time — only R2 does.

  • Find component values that give exactly 1 Hz.
  • Make the LED on-time equal to the off-time.
  • Increase only C1 — what changes?
  • Make the flashing as fast as possible.
  • Which resistor controls the discharge time?

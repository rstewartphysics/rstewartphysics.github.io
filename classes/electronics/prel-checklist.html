How ticks work on this page
Your ticks are saved on this device (in the browser) so you can come back later. They do not replace your booklet evidence. If you’re on a shared device, use the Reset ticks button.

Reset only clears ticks on the website — it does not affect your booklet.

✅ What you must do every lesson (2–3 minute routine)

  1. Date your work (logbook entry or section progress).
  2. Tick what you completed in the booklet (and write a short note if something didn’t work).
  3. Keep evidence clear: labels, values, test results, and repair notes must be readable.
  4. Hand the booklet in at the end of the lesson for teacher check/initials.
What “good evidence” looks like
Clear labels (LED1, R3, IC2…), neat values (4k7, 270Ω), test readings recorded, and repairs explained (what was wrong + what you changed + what test proved it worked).

🗓️ Timeline (Week Beginning targets)

These are “stay on track” targets. Your booklet is checked every lesson.

  • W/B 29 Dec 2025
    Read the brief + understand required behaviour (light/dark + flashing sequences).
  • W/B 05 Jan 2026
    Start Analysis & Design — block diagram + begin Yenka.
  • W/B 12 Jan 2026
    Get Yenka working + start the component list (IDs, values, quantities).
  • W/B 19 Jan 2026
    Complete stripboard layout diagram + add test points + colour wiring lines.
  • W/B 26 Jan 2026
    Deadline: Planning & Analysis finished by end of January.
  • W/B 02 Feb 2026
    Construction begins — build sub-systems and test as you go.
  • W/B 09 Feb 2026
    Continue construction + pre power-up checks + early functionality tests.
  • W/B 23 Feb 2026 onward
    Connect sub-systems + wiring schedule + full testing + repairs.
  • March (all weeks)
    Finish build, fix faults, full system test evidence, logbook kept up to date.
  • Final week before Easter holidays
    Evaluation + final checks + submit.
If you fall behind
Don’t panic — but do act fast: write what’s missing in your logbook, speak to your teacher, and plan the next 1–2 steps you will complete in the next lesson.

⭐ End-of-January “Ready to Build” checkpoint

You should have these completed before construction starts.

  • Block diagram complete + clearly labelled
  • Yenka works exactly as required + labels + test points
  • Component list complete (IDs, values, quantities, supplier/cost info if your booklet requires it)
  • Stripboard layout complete (symbols, track cuts, colours, test points, labels)
  • I know what I’m building first in W/B 02 Feb
Teacher check tip
Make sure your Yenka labels, component IDs, and stripboard labels all match — this makes marking and fault-finding much easier.

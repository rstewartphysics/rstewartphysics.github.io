How ticks work on this page
Your ticks are saved on this device (in the browser) so you can come back later. They do not replace your booklet evidence. If you’re on a shared device, use Reset ticks.

Reset only clears ticks on the website — it does not affect your booklet.

✅ What you must do every lesson (2–3 minute routine)

  1. Date your work (logbook entry or section progress).
  2. Tick what you completed in the booklet (and write a short note if something didn’t work).
  3. Keep evidence clear: labels, values, test results, and repair notes must be readable.
  4. Hand the booklet in at the end of the lesson for teacher check/initials.
What “good evidence” looks like
Clear labels (LED1, R3, IC2…), neat values (4k7, 270Ω), test readings recorded, and repairs explained (what was wrong + what you changed + what test proved it worked).

⭐ End-of-January “Ready to Build” checkpoint

You should have these completed before construction starts.

  • Block diagram complete + clearly labelled
  • Yenka works exactly as required + labels + test points
  • Component list complete (IDs, values, quantities, supplier/cost info if your booklet requires it)
  • Stripboard layout complete (symbols, track cuts, colours, test points, labels)
  • I know what I’m building first in W/B 02 Feb

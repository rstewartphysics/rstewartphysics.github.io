Why this is the answer
SQA marking guidance for this style of question

Six marks are available in the exam:

  • 1 mark — selecting both input devices
  • 1 mark — selecting the correct gates
  • 1 mark — selecting both output devices
  • 1 mark — correctly linking the inputs to the gates
  • 1 mark — correctly linking the gates to the outputs
  • 1 mark — correctly identifying the three sections of the system

Arrows are not required on the connections, but every selected element must be shown as part of a block diagram — a list or a table scores no more than 5. Do not draw logic gate symbols: write the gate name in a box.

This builder gives you the links and the three sections, so it marks the three selection marks. In the exam you draw the boxes, links and section labels yourself.

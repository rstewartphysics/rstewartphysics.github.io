Stripboard planning sheet

Printed at true size — 0.1 inch between holes — so you can lay a real board on top of the grid and the holes line up. Print at 100%, not “fit to page”, then check the bar below with a ruler before you draw on it.

Check the scale first

Measure the bar with a ruler. If it is not exactly 50 mm, the print scaling is wrong and the grid will not match a real board — print again at 100%.

Before you hand it in

  • Every net on the circuit diagram numbered, and that many tracks in use.
  • Every part has an item ID, the same on the layout and the schedule.
  • Every track the chip crosses cut, drawn as an X inside the chip outline.
  • Every leg in the track its net was given — checked track by track.
  • Every link numbered and coloured; red and black used for nothing but the supply.
  • Every gate input held down to 0 V unless another gate drives it.
  • Polarised parts the right way round.
  • A test point on each sub-system input and output, numbered, with its expected reading.
  • Sheets attached to the booklet.

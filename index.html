Overview

Science is how we turn curiosity into evidence — asking brave questions, testing ideas, and discovering patterns in the living and non-living world.

Physics is the hidden rulebook of the universe: from sparks in a wire to stars in a galaxy — the same few ideas explaining wildly different things.

Electronics is where ideas become real: you build systems that sense, decide, and respond — and you learn the superpower of making things work.

Engineering is turning “wouldn’t it be cool if…” into “it works” — designing, building, testing, and improving until it’s genuinely brilliant.
